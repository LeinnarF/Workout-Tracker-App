# Overview

This is the ideation process of my mobile app that will specifically solve MY problem. The app is about a workout tracker that will provide insights. The general idea is that tracking workout progression by pen and paper or note apps is tedious and lacks the ability to give insight on the run. This app will allow me to enter my stats for my workout for easier tracking and progressive overload. This app is specifically made for me.

# Main Features
1. Enter the exercise along with the weight, reps, and sets.
2. Progressive overload helper that will suggest to increase the weight once the target reps per set is achieved.
    - Example: If the user is doing 3 sets of 10 reps with 50 lbs, once they can complete all 3 sets of 10 reps, the app will suggest increasing the weight to 55 lbs for the next workout.
    - Note: The weight increase is up to the user to decide, but the app will provide a suggestion based on their performance.
3. Unit conversion for weight (lbs to kg and vice versa) and distance (miles to kilometers and vice versa).
4. Workout history and progress tracking with visual graphs to show improvements over time.

# My use case
For context, my workout program is a full-body workout 3 times a week, with a focus on compound lifts. I want to track my progress in terms of weight lifted, reps completed, and sets performed. I also want to see how my strength is improving over time and get suggestions for when to increase the weight I'm lifting. Sometimes in my gym the units are in pounds and sometimes in kilograms, so having a unit conversion feature will be very helpful. Additionally, I want to be able to look back at my workout history and see visual representations of my progress, which will help keep me motivated and on track with my fitness goals.

What I want in the interface:
- Minimalistic and user-friendly design that allows for quick data entry during workouts.
- A clear and intuitive layout for viewing workout history and progress graphs.
- Indicators for when to increase weight based on performance.
- Use +/- steppers that move by plate increments, with tap-to-type opening the numeric keypad.
